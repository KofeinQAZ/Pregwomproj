# Pregwomproj
Problem Statement
The problem statement should be concise, meaningful, and answer the following questions: Who? What is the problem? Why is it happening? What does it lead to?

Problem Statement:
Pregnant women experience increased levels of stress and anxiety due to fear of the unknown and the lack of a convenient tool to monitor their condition. The need to keep an action plan in mind, store medical data in separate places, and track physical changes leads to information overload. They need a unified, reassuring digital space that structures pregnancy planning, securely stores personal and medical data, and provides quick access to proven relaxation techniques (breathing, meditation) to restore a sense of control and calm.
